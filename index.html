QUALITY & DOCUMENTATION

Quality begins with
the right questions.

Every commodity has a different purpose. Evaluation should follow the application, agreed specification and the actual batch being purchased.

From specification to sample

Define the product form and intended application before comparing figures. For food ingredients, discuss moisture, appearance, processing and packing. For industrial products, agree the performance or chemical parameters relevant to your process.

Sample evaluation, laboratory analysis and a batch-specific COA can support the decision. A result in a company profile describes the tested sample; it does not automatically apply to every shipment.

Documentation to discuss

Requirements vary with the product, transaction and destination. Confirm the relevant original documents and their validity with our team.

  • Certificate of Analysis (COA)
  • Certificate of Origin (COO)
  • Phytosanitary / fumigation, where applicable
  • MSDS / handling documentation
  • Commercial invoice & packing list
  • Bill of Lading / applicable packing documents
