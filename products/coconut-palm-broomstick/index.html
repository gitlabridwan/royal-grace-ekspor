Overview

Natural palm brooms are made from selected palm-leaf midribs that are dried, sorted, and tied into a practical cleaning product. The portfolio includes natural bundles, finished brooms with handles, and compressed export-bale options.

Benefits

Natural palm midribs

Palm leaf midribs are dried, sorted and hand-tied.

Practical for daily sweeping

Strong, flexible bristles for homes and gardens.

Several bundle formats

Natural bundles, handles and pressed export bales are available to discuss.
