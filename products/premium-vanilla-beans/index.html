Packaging & Buyer Checklist

Packaging & Preparation

The profile lists vacuum-sealed 1 kg and 5 kg packs inside cartons. Confirm pack size, labeling, and the final product condition with the team.

A useful quotation starts with the intended application, required length, moisture range, aroma profile, and packaging. The supplied videos show pods at different handling and preparation stages; their appearance should not be treated as proof of a finished grade. Agree the curing stage and batch specification before placing an order.

What to Include in Your Inquiry

  • Gourmet beans or extraction material?
  • Required length and moisture range?
  • Preferred pack size and labeling?
  • Sample evaluation and purchase timeline?

From Requirements to Selection

  1. Confirm the intended application and required grade.
  2. Discuss curing stage, pod length, moisture, and vanillin requirements.
  3. Evaluate a sample and agree the batch specification.
  4. Confirm vacuum-pack sizes, carton requirements, and delivery terms.
